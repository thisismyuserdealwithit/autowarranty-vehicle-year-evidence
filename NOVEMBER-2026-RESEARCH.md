## How 12 Vehicle Service Contract Forms Define Labor Payments

[Read the report](https://autowarranty.autos/research/warranty-contract-labor-audit) · [Download CSV](https://autowarranty.autos/research/warranty-contract-labor-audit/data) · [Download JSON](https://autowarranty.autos/research/warranty-contract-labor-audit/data?format=json)

The audit covers 12 distinct public specimen PDFs from three marketers: three Endurance forms, six CarShield term forms including EV, and three Omega Auto Care forms. All 12 refer to a labor guide. Three use the shop's published hourly rate, six use an administrator-determined regional rate, and three do not separately state an hourly-rate rule in the base payment paragraph. An unstated rule does not mean unlimited payment.

The 12-row download records form identifiers, source URLs, PDF hashes, one-based PDF page positions, and observations about labor rates, allowed hours, deductibles and payout limits. It can be used to check a classification against the cited document. Shared PDFs count once; different PDFs can still share substantial wording.

This is a non-random sample of document wording, not a market survey or evidence of claims paid, approval rates or customer outcomes. State endorsements, optional coverage and a customer's completed agreement can change the terms. Official specimen PDFs are linked, not republished here.

## What Changed in NHTSA Records After Our October Snapshot?

[Read the report](https://autowarranty.autos/research/vehicle-record-changes-2026-11) · [Download CSV](https://autowarranty.autos/research/vehicle-record-changes-2026-11/data) · [Download JSON](https://autowarranty.autos/research/vehicle-record-changes-2026-11/data?format=json)

This comparison retains 2,742 model-years across 323 models, using the corrected October 18 baseline and archives retrieved November 10, 2026. Of those rows, 2,649 have supported source-name matches at both dates. Complaint counts increased for 1,254 comparable model-years; recall-campaign counts increased for 57. Three previously unmatched rows gained a match and are excluded from the change totals; 90 remain unmatched.

The CSV preserves both match statuses, counts, differences and campaign IDs added or removed from each comparable model-year. The JSON adds scope and source provenance. Blank differences mean that a row cannot support the comparison. Manufacturer communications were not refreshed or compared.

These are changes in recorded counts, not counts of new incidents or failure rates. The baseline does not preserve individual complaint-ID sets, so the report cannot identify which complaint IDs appeared or disappeared. Its corrected October population is not interchangeable with this repository's August snapshot. Repeating the source joins requires the retained source files; current NHTSA URLs may serve later versions.
